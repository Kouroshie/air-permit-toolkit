Sources used to build the tool

Every rule, manual, dataset and reference the toolkit's values come from, what each one supplies, and where to find it. All links were checked on November 15, 2026.

Rule versions: Reg 2-2 (12/6/2017), Reg 2-5 (12/15/2021), Reg 3 (adopted 6/3/2026, effective 7/1/2026)

What the Air District website already offers

A review of baaqmd.gov on November 15, 2026 found no single web calculator for permit review. The public calculation resources are the Permit Handbook (a 316-page PDF), per-category Excel spreadsheets linked from it, the CEQA Health Risk Calculator 5.0 spreadsheet, screening maps, and an example engine fee sheet. The toolkit does not replace any of them. It puts the public methods side by side, in the browser, with the arithmetic shown.

Google Deep ReviewAn AI review of the methods paper (research.google/deepreview, Nov 15, 2026). It found the series control error, now fixed; two citations it flagged were grouped references, not fabrications, and were split for clarity.
