Air Permit Engineering Toolkit

Work through the numbers in an air permit application: emission rates, BACT and offset triggers, toxics screening, health risk and permit fees. Every result shows its arithmetic, units and source.

Built around Bay Area Air District rules (Regulations 2-2, 2-5 and 3). Independent open-source project, not affiliated with or endorsed by the Air District. Runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Screening and teaching tool. Final permit decisions rest on the current rule text, Air District procedures and an engineer's review. Check the current rules before relying on any threshold or fee.

MIT licensed. Source, methods and tests: github.com/Kouroshie/air-permit-toolkit. Built by Seyed Kourosh Mahjour, PhD. Companion to the Petroleum Engineering Toolkit.
